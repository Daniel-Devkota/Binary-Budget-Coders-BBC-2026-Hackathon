## Slide 1 — Cold Open Skit `0:00 – 0:30`

No slide, no logo yet — just the skit. This is the hook: fast and a little funny. Two teammates on camera, phone selfie-style or webcam.

**Alex** *(holding a steaming pitcher, staring at milky foam disaster)*: Tutorial #14 on "Steaming Milk." My latte art looks like a cloudy puddle again.

**Jordan**: Still drowning your kitchen in milk?

**Alex**: I just need someone standing next to me to show me the exact angle to tilt the pitcher. Not another slow-mo barista video.

**Jordan**: So book a barista workshop?

**Alex**: For basic foam? That's $150 for a two-hour class.

**Jordan**: Fair. …You know I worked at a specialty cafe for three years, right?

**Alex** *(pause)*: …You've watched me waste two gallons of oat milk.

**Jordan** *(grins, pulls out phone)*: I know. But there's a better way to do this. *(beat)* …SkillUp.

*Whip pan / zoom into phone screen — title card slams in.*

> **On screen:** "Learn anything. From someone nearby. For real."

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem `0:30 – 0:45`

**Visual:** Simple icon row — isolated learner watching a video / a price tag next to a tutor icon / no local human connection.

**Script**

> "You're stuck between two bad options: impersonal, one-size-fits-all videos, or hands-on classes that can cost a hundred and fifty dollars for a single afternoon. Meanwhile, the person who could teach you in twenty minutes for free might live down the street — you just have no way to find them."

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution: The Skill Swap `0:45 – 1:05`

**Visual:** App logo + core mechanic diagram — two profile cards ("Can Teach: Guitar" / "Wants to Learn: Guitar") sliding together into a matched pair.

**Script**

> "SkillUp is built around one core idea: everyone has something to teach, and something they want to learn. You list both — the skills you can pass on, and the skills you're chasing — and our matching engine pairs you up with people nearby whose teach list matches your learn list, and vice versa. That's the swap. And because it's person-to-person, it's not just a transaction — it's a real connection with someone in your community, sometimes across cultures and backgrounds you'd never otherwise cross paths with."

---

## Slide 4 — Onboarding: Set Your Skills `1:05 – 1:20`

**Visual:** Screen recording — profile setup showing two lists side by side: "I can teach" / "I want to learn," plus availability + location.

**Script**

> "Onboarding is quick: set what you can teach, what you want to learn, your availability, and your location for in-person matches — or mark yourself open to online sessions."

---

## Slide 5 — The Match `1:20 – 1:40`

**Visual:** Screen recording — the app surfacing a matched swap (not a search results list): "You teach Photography, they teach Latte Art — matched!"

**Script**

> "Instead of just browsing a list, SkillUp actively matches you — surfacing people whose skills complement yours. You can still search for a specific skill directly, but the swap is what makes it click: you're not just booking a class, you're trading knowledge."

**Callout on screen:** "Group sessions are capped 1-in-5 — keeps exchanges intimate, not lecture halls."

---

## Slide 6 — The Exchange + Proof `1:40 – 2:00`

**Visual:** Screen recording — booking confirmation (tokens spent to book) → mock "session complete" photo capture screen.

**Script**

> "Booking a session costs tokens; teaching one earns them back — plus everyone gets a small weekly token allowance just for being active. After the session — in person or on a call — you snap a photo together as proof of the exchange. That photo becomes part of your permanent skill history."

---

## Slide 7 — Map `2:00 – 2:15`

**Visual:** Screen recording — zoom from world map → country → city view.

**Script**

> "The map lets you zoom from a global view all the way down to your own city, so you can see exactly who's teaching or offering swaps near you right now."

---

## Slide 8 — Social Feed & Profile `2:15 – 2:40`

**Visual:** Screen recording — Feed page (friends' completed sessions) → Profile page with skill albums.

**Script**

> "Your profile becomes a visual record — photo albums organized by skill, showing your whole learning journey. And your feed shows what your friends have been learning and who taught them — so skill-building becomes social, not solitary. Every swap is also a small thread connecting neighbors and communities who might never have met otherwise."

---

## Slide 9 — Closing / CTA `2:40 – 3:00`

**Visual:** Full app logo, team names, tech stack line, "Built at [Hackathon Name]".

**Script**

> "That's SkillUp — turning your neighborhood into a classroom, one swap at a time. Thanks for watching!"

*Optional: cut back to Alex & Jordan, thumbs up to camera, for a callback laugh.*

---

## Appendix — Production Notes

- Keep the skit under 30 seconds — judges score substance; the skit just needs to earn attention.
- Record each flow (search → book → photo → map → feed) as separate clean clips so you can cut tightly to the script timing.
- Short on build time? Mock the "session complete" photo screen with placeholder UI — it doesn't need to be functional for the video.
- Use a consistent lower-third caption style (feature name in the corner) during screen recordings so judges can follow along on mute.
